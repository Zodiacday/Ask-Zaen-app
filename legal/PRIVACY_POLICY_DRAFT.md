# Ask Zaen Privacy Policy — Product Draft

**Effective date:** [Insert publication date]

**Important:** This is a product draft for review, not legal advice. It must be checked against the final Expo build, Clerk configuration, hosting/logging configuration, and the laws of the markets where Ask Zaen is offered.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

Ask Zaen is operated by **Natan Selomon**, an independent developer. For privacy questions or requests, contact **asteriuselos@gmail.com**.

For the purposes of applicable data-protection law, Natan Selomon is responsible for the personal data processed for Ask Zaen, except where a third-party provider acts independently as its own controller.

## 2. What Ask Zaen is designed to collect

Ask Zaen is a parent-facing service. Guest browsing does not require an account.

If you create an account, we may process:

- your account identifier from the authentication provider;
- your name or display name, if the provider makes it available;
- your email address, including a private relay address where Apple provides one;
- sign-in and session information needed to authenticate you;
- Ask Zaen preferences, such as language, age tier, and selected subscriptions or channels;
- saved media IDs and playlist information, including playlist titles, emoji, and ordering;
- state revision and update timestamps used to synchronize your data safely.

We do not intentionally request or require a child’s name, exact birth date, location, device identifier, or child account.

## 3. Technical and security information

When you use an online service, technical information may be processed by Ask Zaen, its hosting provider, authentication provider, or security systems. Depending on the final configuration, this may include IP address, request timestamps, browser or device information, crash information, and security or diagnostic logs.

The final published version of this policy must be updated to match the actual server, SDK, analytics, crash-reporting, and logging configuration. We will not use technical data to build child profiles.

## 4. How we use personal data

We use personal data to:

- provide sign-in, account recovery, and session management;
- synchronize preferences, saved titles, and playlists across devices;
- prevent unauthorized access, abuse, fraud, and security incidents;
- respond to support requests;
- maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the reliability of the Service;
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use parent account data to create behavioral profiles of children or to deliver behavioral advertising to children.

## 5. Legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, we generally rely on:

- performance of a contract or steps requested before creating an account, for account and synchronization features;
- legitimate interests, for security, abuse prevention, service reliability, and support, balanced against your rights;
- legal obligations, where processing is required by law;
- consent, where consent is legally required for a specific optional activity.

The precise legal basis for a future feature will be identified before that feature is introduced.

## 6. Providers and sharing

We may share the minimum necessary information with service providers that help operate Ask Zaen, including:

- Clerk, for authentication, account management, and private account metadata;
- hosting, infrastructure, and security providers, for operating the API and application;
- Apple or Google, if you choose their sign-in method;
- external media services, when you choose to open a third-party link from Ask Zaen.

These providers may process data in countries outside Sweden or the European Economic Area. Before publication, we will identify the actual providers, processing locations, transfer safeguards, and retention periods used by the production configuration.

We may disclose information where required by law, to protect the Service or another person, or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

## 7. Children’s privacy

Ask Zaen is designed for parents and caregivers rather than child accounts. We do not intentionally collect personal information directly from children and do not require child personal information for the Service.

Parents should not enter child names, exact birth dates, locations, health information, or other child personal information into account fields, playlist titles, support requests, or free-text areas. If you believe a child’s personal data has been submitted, contact **asteriuselos@gmail.com** so we can assess and remove it where appropriate.

## 8. Retention and deletion

We retain account and synchronized state while your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify the account data and Ask Zaen state that we control, except where limited retention is required for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or backup integrity.

Authentication-provider records may also be subject to the provider’s deletion process and policy. The in-app account-deletion flow is intended to initiate deletion of the Ask Zaen account and associated state.

Technical and security logs are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for their purpose, subject to the final production configuration and applicable legal requirements.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live and which law applies, you may have rights to:

- access your personal data;
- correct inaccurate data;
- request deletion;
- request restriction of processing;
- object to certain processing;
- receive portable data;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
- complain to a supervisory authority.

To make a request, email **asteriuselos@gmail.com**. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. You may also contact the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) or the supervisory authority in your country.

## 10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the data and the Service. No online service can guarantee absolute security. You should use a unique password, protect your sign-in methods, and notify us promptly about suspected unauthorized access.

## 11. External services and links

Ask Zaen may link to third-party media platforms. When you open one of those services, that service may collect data under its own privacy policy, including information about your device, activity, or account. Ask Zaen does not control third-party privacy practices.

## 12. International availability

Ask Zaen may be made available internationally. This policy is intended to provide a GDPR-aware baseline and will be supplemented by any rights or notices required in the country where you use the Service.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our data practices, providers, features, or legal obligations change. The updated version will show a new effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
